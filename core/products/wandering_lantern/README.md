# Path of the Wandering Lantern: Production & Illustration Guide
**A Sequential Manga Coloring Book for Adults**  
**Target Platform:** Amazon KDP Print-on-Demand  
**Format:** 8.5" x 11" Paperback | Single-Sided Right Pages | 24 Narrative Scenes | 52 Total Pages  

---

## 1. Book Overview & KDP Technical Specifications

- **Trim Size:** 8.5" x 11.0" (215.9 mm x 279.4 mm)
- **Binding:** Perfect Bound Paperback
- **Interior Color / Paper:** Black & White on 55–60 lb White Paper
- **Bleed Settings:** No Bleed (0.375" outer margins, 0.5" gutter margin)
- **Margin Safe Zones:**
  - **Inside Gutter Margin:** Minimum 0.5" (12.7 mm) to prevent artwork disappearing into the spine.
  - **Outside, Top, Bottom Margins:** Minimum 0.375" (9.5 mm).
- **Line Art Density & Style Guidelines:**
  - **Line Weight:** 1.5 pt to 3.0 pt vector strokes. Hairline rules below 0.75 pt will break up on KDP presses.
  - **Negative Space:** Open silhouettes and spacious interior fills. Avoid large solid black fills (>10% of total page area).
  - **Shading Strategy:** Discrete cross-hatching or light contour lines only; allow colorists room for colored pencil gradients and alcohol marker blending.
- **Facing (Left-Hand) Page Structure:**
  - Left pages carry the chapter title, narrative lore/vignette, color mood suggestions, and a reminder to place a blotter sheet behind the coloring page.
  - Right pages contain the hero illustration.

## 3. Narrative Breakdown & Scene Catalog (Scenes 1–24)

- **Scene 11: Blade & Silk** (Ren parrying silk cords in spinning defense; paper lanterns tumble and ignite).
- **Scene 12: The Mirror Cavern** (Underground amethyst crystal lake; reflection reveals deceased samurai master).

- **Scene 17: The Dragon Ascent** (Colossal Ryū water dragon spiraling around a needle peak beside cliff stairs).

### Act III: The Celestial Shrine & The Dawn Climax (Scenes 19–24)
