### Candidate 1 (BBQ-001): Error-Avoiding Quantum Compilation

- **Candidate Identifier**: `BBQ-001`
- **Domain**: Quantum Information Science & Fault-Tolerant Compilation
- **Governing State**: `ADVERSARIAL_TEST` (In Evaluation)
- **External Prior Art**:

- **Hypothesis**: 
  Instead of treating quantum error correction as an exclusively physical hardware problem, a compiler can dynamically search for mathematically equivalent computational representations of an observable whose error manifold is orthogonal to the dominant physical noise channels of the physical QPU.
- **Novel Departure**: 
  The compiler is granted the mathematical degree of freedom to reformulate the underlying Hamiltonian or problem representation itself, rather than merely synthesizing and routing circuits.
- **Physical & Engineering Constraints**:
  - Exact unitary equivalence must be preserved ($\hat{U}^\dagger \hat{H} \hat{U} \equiv \hat{H}'$).
  - Classical search overhead for reformulation must remain sub-exponential ($\mathcal{O}(\text{poly}(N))$).
  - Physical decoherence time ($T_1, T_2$) of the target hardware remains fixed.
- **Hostile Q&A Discernment**:
  - *Q (Vance)*: "Isn't this just dressed-up zero-noise extrapolation or standard error mitigation?"
    - *A (Clara)*: "No. Standard error mitigation scales noise artificially or post-selects measurements. Error-avoiding compilation alters the mathematical coordinate system of the observable prior to pulse generation so that physical noise vectors align with degenerate or insensitive eigenspaces."
  - *Q (Vance)*: "Doesn't searching the space of equivalent mathematical representations explode classically?"
    - *A (Maxx)*: "Yes, if unconstrained. The search must be restricted to Lie-algebraic symmetries and Clifford group invariants generated on local classical M4 silicon."
- **Explicit Kill Conditions**:
  1. If adaptive mathematical reformulation yields less than a $2\sigma$ statistical improvement in output fidelity across a benchmark suite of 100 randomly sampled noisy Hamiltonians compared to standard Qiskit/Braket circuit synthesis.
  2. If the classical reformulation time exceeds the physical decoherence advantage gained on the QPU.
- **Black-Box Experimental Specification**:
  - Blind evaluation across 100 unseen chemistry and lattice Hamiltonians. The compiler is blinded to which representation is expected to perform best.
  - Metric: $\Delta \mathcal{F} = \text{Fidelity}_{\text{adaptive}} - \text{Fidelity}_{\text{standard}}$ per physical two-qubit gate.
- **Known Limitations & Non-Claims**:
  - Does NOT claim to eliminate physical decoherence.
  - Does NOT eliminate the eventual need for fault-tolerant surface codes at scale.
  - Does NOT provide infinite Hilbert space reformulation without classical pre-computation limits.

- **Governing State**: `ADVERSARIAL_TEST` (In Evaluation)

### Candidate 3 (BBQ-003): Multi-Timescale Infrastructure-State Long-Duration Energy Storage

- **Candidate Identifier**: `BBQ-003`
- **Domain**: Grid-Scale Energy Storage & Industrial Infrastructure
- **Governing State**: `UNDER_DELIBERATION` (In Deliberation)
- **External Prior Art**:
  - DOE Long-Duration Energy Storage (LDES) National Target ($0.05/kWh, 10+ hour continuous discharge).
  - ARPA-E DAYS (Duration Addition to electricitY Storage) 10–100 hour program.
- **Hypothesis**: 
  Storing electrical energy solely as electricity-in / electricity-out through electrochemical batteries is economically unviable for 100+ hour multi-day lulls. Capital cost can be minimized by storing energy as reversible physical transformations in civil infrastructure (e.g., pumped chemical feedstocks, pressurized geomechanical reservoirs, low-cost thermal mass, and reversible rust oxidation), dispatched across asymmetric time tiers.
- **Novel Departure**: 
  Abandons the optimization of single-cell round-trip efficiency (RTE) in favor of multi-timescale temporal arbitrage:
  - *Seconds to Minutes*: High-efficiency flywheel / supercapacitor buffer.
  - *Hours to Day*: Sodium-ion / thermal storage.
  - *Days to Weeks*: Low-efficiency, ultra-low-capex reversible iron oxidation ($<\$15/\text{kWh}$).
- **Physical & Engineering Constraints**:
  - Thermodynamic Carnot efficiency limits on heat recovery ($\eta_{\text{thermal}} \le 1 - T_C/T_H$).
  - Chemical parasitic self-discharge and hydrogen evolution in aqueous iron anodes.
  - Footprint and geographic siting constraints.
- **Hostile Q&A Discernment**:
  - *Q (Vance)*: "Isn't a 50% round-trip efficiency battery simply throwing away half the nation's renewable power?"
    - *A (Maxx)*: "During deep renewable curtailment (negative pricing periods), the marginal cost of excess power is zero. A 50% efficient storage system costing \$15/kWh produces dramatically cheaper delivered levelized cost of storage (LCOS) than an 85% efficient lithium battery costing \$250/kWh."
  - *Q (Vance)*: "How does the dispatch optimizer prevent cycling degradation on the multi-day reservoir?"
    - *A (Maxx)*: "Strict tier gating. Fast transients are absorbed entirely by the high-cycle buffer; the chemical reservoir is only engaged when weather forecasting indicates a multi-diurnal deficit."
- **Explicit Kill Conditions**:
  1. Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS) exceeding \$0.08/kWh over a standardized 168-hour extreme winter storm profile (Dunkelflaute event).
  2. Parasitic standby capacity loss exceeding 0.5% per day in the long-duration chemical reservoir.
- **Black-Box Experimental Specification**:
  - Feed the automated dispatch engine a 168-hour empirical grid load and renewable generation profile with a fixed capital expenditure cap. The optimizer must maintain 99.999% grid reliability without human intervention.
- **Known Limitations & Non-Claims**:
  - Does NOT claim high round-trip efficiency for the multi-day chemical phase ($50\text{--}55\%\text{ expected}$).
  - Does NOT serve as an electric vehicle or portable power density solution (strictly stationary grid infrastructure).
