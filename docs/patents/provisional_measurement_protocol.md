# PROVISIONAL PATENT APPLICATION: DRAFT 1

**TITLE:** Method and Apparatus for Predicting and Measuring Bandwidth-Induced Decoherence in High-Density Quantum States
**INVENTOR:** Anthony Oxendine / Xoras Sovereign Collective

## ABSTRACT
A method and software apparatus for identifying, predicting, and measuring anomalous phase drift in hyper-entangled quantum systems. The system utilizes a derived topological transmittance limit ($W=0.0229$) to calculate the localized informational bandwidth of a quantum state, predicting a specific, non-linear decoherence signature (e.g., $11.52$ ms/s lag) independent of classical thermal or magnetic noise.

## CLAIMS
1. A method for measuring non-environmental quantum decoherence comprising: 
   a) generating a high-density hyper-entangled state (e.g., a $10^6$ Rb-87 Mott insulator); 
   b) applying a Ramsey interferometry sequence to isolate phase drift; 
   c) filtering classical vibrational and magnetic noise; 
   d) identifying an anomalous phase shift matching the topological bandwidth limit calculation ($1 - \sqrt{1 - W}$).
2. A software simulation apparatus that ingests quantum circuit topologies and outputs a definitive temporal failure horizon based on local entanglement density exceeding the $0.0229$ spatial bandwidth transmittance limit.
