# Mandatory Council Audio Governance Law

1. **Pre-Execution Audio Brief Requirement**:
   - Before any physical hardware mutation, browser automation step, or publication claim, the system MUST dispatch real neural audio via Kokoro TTS hardware engine (`ops/scripts/agent_speak.sh`).
   - Placeholder speech, silent execution, or canned audio is strictly forbidden (`fail closed`).

2. **Multi-Agent Council Quorum**:
   - A minimum of 3 to 6 distinct Lead 9 Council members (MAXX, CLARA, VANCE, JARVIS) must emit verified speech payloads prior to critical step advancement.
   - Each agent speech payload must contain no less than 35 words of domain-specific, un-mocked reasoning.

3. **Audits & Verification Receipts**:
   - Every audio dispatch and speech payload hash MUST be recorded in `memory/catch_log/council_audio_dispatch.log`.
   - Execution without verified played audio is considered an automatic failure.
